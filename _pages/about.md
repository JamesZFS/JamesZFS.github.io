I am a PhD candidate starting in 2024 at the [Computer Graphics and Visualization group](https://www.tudelft.nl/ewi/over-de-faculteit/afdelingen/intelligent-systems/computer-graphics-and-visualization), TU Delft.

My background is in physically based and [real-time](https://www.shadertoy.com/user/JamesZFS/) rendering. I worked on an [adaptive spatial subdivision technique](/publication/2026-07-01-spatial) for path guiding, in a collaboration with [Disney Research Zurich](https://studios.disneyresearch.com/) and [Blender Institute](https://www.blender.org/about/institute/). Prior to this, I explored various rendering-related topics including compilers, denoising, and data-driven methods.

In my free time, I love making music through different instruments and singing with people. I want to explore how to combine music and visualization in a creative way, fostering an engaging and rewarding experience for choir beginners to learn singing. For a proof of concept, I made [Seeing Harmony](/lissajous/index.html), an interval progression visualizer.

I obtained my master's degree at ETH Zurich and my bachelor's degree at Tsinghua University. I was a graphics programming intern at Bytedance Game Department in the summer of 2020, and a research intern at the [Realistic Graphics Lab](https://rgl.epfl.ch/), EPFL in the summer of 2022.
